Terms of use

These are the rules for using ChitBridge for your shop, in plain words.

Your account

  • You tell us the truth about your shop and your country. The country decides your tax setup.
  • You keep your sign-in safe. What is done with it is done by you.
  • Tell us if a phone or PC is lost, and sign it out in Settings.

Your records

  • Your bills, chits and books are yours. We keep them for you and do not sell them.
  • You are responsible for what you enter and for the tax you file. We help you keep the records.
  • A sealed chit is not edited. A mistake is fixed by a new entry beside it.

Fair use

  • Use ChitBridge only for lawful trade. Do not try to reach another shop's records.
  • We may pause an account that puts others or the service at risk, and we say why.

The service

  • We work to keep it running, but it can have faults or stops. Keep your own copy of anything you must have.
  • When we change these terms, we show you the new ones before you go on.

Read what we keep about you

Privacy

What we keep, why we keep it, and who else sees it.

What we keep

  • Your name, phone, e-mail and sign-ins, to run your account and keep it safe.
  • Your shop's records: chits, bills, books, parties and notes, because that is the service you asked for.
  • For your staff, only what you enter for them. For your customers, only what you or they give.
  • Who agreed to be contacted, and when, so a message is sent only to people who said yes.
  • Technical logs (your address and browser), to keep the service safe.

How long

  • Your account and records stay while your shop is with us. You decide what to remove.
  • Some trade records must be kept for a set time by the tax rules of your country. We keep those until then.

Who else sees it

  • Our database and hosting providers, and the sender of your sign-in codes by e-mail or SMS.
  • The other party on a chit sees their own copy of it. Nobody else does.
  • WhatsApp, if you turn on order updates. The AI helpers, only for the text or picture you send them.

Where it is kept

Today your data is kept in India. For a shop in another country, that means it is kept abroad.

Your say

  • You can ask to see, fix or remove what we hold about you.
  • Ask your shop. It passes the request on to us, and we answer in the time your country allows.
